What the model sees (8 x 8 pixels)

Model: neural network (64-64-10) trained with scikit-learn on distorted digit scans and simulated pen strokes. Accuracy on held-out tests: about 98% on standard scans and about 89% on strongly distorted simulated pen strokes. It works best with one digit drawn large, in the centre. Everything runs locally in your browser; no drawing is sent anywhere.
